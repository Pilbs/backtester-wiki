Forex Backtester

Backtest Engine

What the Engine Does

The engine takes completed strategy candles, execution candles, a strategy, account assumptions and execution assumptions, then deterministically simulates orders, fills, trades and account state.

strategy decisions
      |
      v
trade intents
      |
      v
orders -> fills -> trades
      |
      v
account / margin / risk
      |
      v
structured backtest result

Account Model

The account layer tracks starting capital, balance, equity, realised/unrealised P&L, leverage, margin used/free, peak equity and drawdown. It can enforce max open trades, trades per side, position units, gross exposure, margin usage, drawdown and daily-loss limits.

Position modes are HEDGING and NETTING.

Position Sizing

UNITS            fixed units
CASH             fixed notional cash amount
PERCENT_EQUITY   notional position as % of equity
RISK_PERCENT     units derived from equity risk + stop distance

RISK_PERCENT requires a stop. For example, value 1 means size the trade so a stop-out is approximately 1% of current equity.

Orders and Lifecycle

Order types: MARKET | LIMIT | STOP | STOP_LIMIT
Time in force: GTC | DAY | IOC | FOK
Actions: ENTER | EXIT | UPDATE_STOP | UPDATE_TARGET | CANCEL_ORDER

The engine supports multiple simultaneous trades, partial exits, strategy-controlled exits, dynamic stop/target changes and pending-order cancellation/expiry.

Price and Execution Model

Strategies normally calculate on MID. Execution uses the correct spread side:

LONG  enter ASK, exit BID
SHORT enter BID, exit ASK

Slippage and commission are configurable. Commission models include none, pips per side, fixed per order and percent notional.

Deterministic OHLC Rules

OHLC bars do not reveal the exact order of every intrabar event. The engine therefore uses explicit reproducible rules rather than pretending tick detail exists.

  • A strategy sees a candle only after that candle closes.
  • If stop and target are both touched in one execution candle, STOP_FIRST or TARGET_FIRST determines the assumption.
  • An intrabar entry cannot retrospectively use the whole entry candle for protection or MFE/MAE.
  • An intrabar protective exit cannot include later movement in that candle in MFE/MAE.
  • Stop-limit activation is conservative: activation and a later limit fill are not invented from unknowable same-bar ordering.

Results and Diagnostics

Completed trades carry P&L, exit reason, holding time, causal/conservative MFE and MAE, and whether the trade was ever profitable. Summaries include trade counts, win rate, pips/account P&L, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, year/month breakdowns and rejection/risk counts.

Deliberate V1 Limits

The engine does not claim tick/order-book precision, dynamic historical account-currency conversion or synchronized multi-instrument execution. Those are explicit scope boundaries, not hidden assumptions.
