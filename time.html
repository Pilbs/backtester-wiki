Forex Backtester

Time & Sessions

Candle Timestamps

OANDA candle timestamps represent the candle start. An M5 candle stamped 09:15 therefore completes at 09:20. The strategy must not see its final OHLC until 09:20.

Strategy vs Execution Timeframe

strategy timeframe:  M5  -> when decisions are made
execution timeframe: M1  -> how fills/stops/targets are simulated

The strategy still only receives completed M5 candles. M1 data gives the engine finer sequencing after a signal is actually known.

Timezone Model

Stored candle timestamps remain UTC. Strategy session logic converts them into an IANA timezone such as America/New_York or Europe/London, so daylight-saving transitions are handled by timezone rules rather than hard-coded UTC offsets.

summer: 12:15 UTC = 08:15 New York
winter: 13:15 UTC = 08:15 New York

Session Windows

The generic session helper classifies a timestamp relative to a local start time and duration as BEFORE, IN_SESSION or AFTER. ORB uses this utility for its opening range; other strategies can reuse it.

{
    startHour: 8,
    startMinute: 15,
    durationMinutes: 60,
    timeZone: "America/New_York"
}

Why This Matters

Time handling is part of backtest correctness, not presentation. The engine's causal rule is: a decision can only use information that would have existed at that moment.

This is also why the final MFE/MAE implementation does not use unknowable pre-entry or post-exit movement from an intrabar candle.

Account Risk Timezone

Account config also has riskTimeZone (default UTC). This controls boundaries for daily risk calculations such as max daily loss; it is separate from a strategy's own session timezone.
