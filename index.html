Core Principles

  • Market data is stored once in Cloudflare D1 and reused.
  • Strategy calculations use MID; execution uses BID / ASK.
  • A strategy cannot see a candle before that candle is complete.
  • Strategy timeframe and execution timeframe can differ.
  • The engine understands executable trade instructions, not ORB-specific concepts.
  • Same inputs and configuration should produce the same result.

Long-Term Goal

Turn strategy ideas into repeatable experiments over years of market data, then let parameter sweeps and eventually AI drive the research loop.

Pine ingestion remains a later layer. The immediate focus is structured strategy configuration and research tooling.
